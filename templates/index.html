ABOUT RAILCONNECT

More Than Just a Railway Reservation System

RailConnect is an integrated railway management platform designed to simplify the passenger journey through a unified digital experience. From train searches and ticket reservations to live train status and onboard food ordering, RailConnect brings essential railway services together in one place.

Smart Reservations

Search trains, book tickets and manage cancellations through a simple reservation system.

Train Tracking

Explore train routes and check simulated train-running-status information through an interactive dashboard.

E-Catering

Browse available food options, manage your cart and place food orders for your journey.

Unified Management

Access essential railway services through one integrated platform.
